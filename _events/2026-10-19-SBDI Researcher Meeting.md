**Background:** The Swedish Biodiversity Data Infrastructure (SBDI) is preparing to apply to the Swedish Research Council (VR) research infrastructure of national interest call in early 2027. As part of the preparations, SBDI will hold Researcher Meetings with relevant researcher groups across Sweden during 2026.

**Purpose:** The purpose of the meetings is to better understand the needs of current and potential users of SBDI and to identify important areas for future development. Discussions will focus on how researchers use biodiversity data and the infrastructure today, where there are gaps or barriers, and what services, data, tools or support could strengthen future research.

This meeting is open for anyone interested. We will be offering a light lunch for those who have registered by the 14th of October.
