This will be a free lunch-to-lunch meeting (including lunches, fika, and a joined dinner) with a focus on **community building**, **knowledge exchange** and **identifying community support priorities** for Sweden’s eDNA and metabarcoding research community (see detailed program under the link above). We will also have an external speaker Tiina Laamanen who will give us some inspiration about how the eDNA community is organized in our neighbor country Finland.
 
Registration deadline is November 30th! Spaces are limited (currently to 60). So, sign up as soon as possible [here](https://metabarcoding.se/meeting-2026/)!
Please spread the word in your institutions, newsletters, and within your network.
If you’re a researcher based in Sweden and are not a member yet, you can easily become one by signing up (for free) on our [webpage](https://metabarcoding.se/).
 
SMN is a SciLifeLab-funded Research Data Community Project.
