SBDI is preparing an application for the Swedish Research Council's (VR) research infrastructure call in early 2027. To help shape the future development of SBDI, we are gathering input from current and potential users through a series of researcher meetings and a user survey during 2026.

**Your feedback is important!** The survey aims to identify user needs, priorities, and opportunities for improving SBDI's services and infrastructure.

Whether you are already using SBDI or considering doing so in the future, we encourage you to share your views.
