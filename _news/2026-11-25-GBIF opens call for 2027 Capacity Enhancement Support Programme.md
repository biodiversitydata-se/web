GBIF has opened its 2027 call for proposals under the **Capacity Enhancement Support Programme (CESP)**, offering funding for collaborative projects that strengthen skills, partnerships and capacity for sharing and using biodiversity data across the GBIF network.

CESP supports international collaboration between GBIF participant nodes to address identified capacity needs. Eligible activities include mentoring between nodes, regional training workshops, and the development or adaptation of guidance and training materials. Projects should deliver lasting benefits for participating institutions and the wider GBIF community.

The application process begins with a short concept note submitted through the GBIF Grants Portal. Researchers and institutions in Sweden interested in developing international collaborations through CESP are encouraged to contact GBIF Sweden to discuss potential project ideas and partnerships.

Full details, including eligibility criteria, funding conditions, application deadlines and instructions, are available in the official call:

[Call for proposals for 2027 Capacity Enhancement Support Programme](https://www.gbif.org/news/342v2yvyNBNEkHzDcNQQCZ/call-for-proposals-for-2027-capacity-enhancement-support-programme)
